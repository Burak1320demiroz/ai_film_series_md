ANALYZE THE FOLLOWING, ALWAYS WITH EXACT TIMESTAMPS

1. HOOK (0–3 s): Does the first frame and the first 3 seconds stop the scroll? Score it from 1 to 10 and explain why. Give 3 concrete ways to make it stronger.

2. RETENTION RISK MAP: Go through the episode second by second and mark every moment where a viewer is likely to swipe away (slow moments, confusing cuts, weak lines, repeated information). Explain why for each one.

3. STORY CLARITY: Imagine a viewer who knows nothing about the series. Within the first 15 seconds, can they understand who Kerem is, who Selin and Nevin are, how they are related and why he is being humiliated? Is the flash-forward clear, or confusing? What is missing?

4. CHARACTER CONSISTENCY: Compare every appearance of each character across all clips. Flag any moment where a face, body type, hair, outfit, jewelry or age looks different from the other clips or from the descriptions above. Be very specific (for example: "Selin's hair is shorter at 32 s than at 12 s").

5. AI ARTIFACTS AND VISUAL ERRORS: Find every glitch: distorted or extra fingers, morphing faces, melting objects, unnatural body movements, broken physics, flickering, objects appearing or disappearing, wrong or misspelled text on the phone screen, characters who look at the wrong place, people in the background behaving strangely.

6. AUDIO AND DIALOGUE: Check every line of dialogue. Is it clear and understandable? Is the lip-sync correct? Does each character's voice stay consistent across clips? Are any lines cut off, too fast, robotic, or spoken by the wrong character? Is the volume balanced between dialogue, sound effects and any music? List every problem with its timestamp.

7. PACING AND EDITING: Is each clip the right length? Which clips feel too long or too short? Are the cuts and transitions smooth? Suggest exact trims (for example: "cut 0.5 s from the end of Clip 4").

8. EMOTIONAL IMPACT: Does the humiliation feel strong enough to make viewers angry and feel sympathy for Kerem? Which moment is the emotional peak? Which moments feel flat or fake? Is Selin's betrayal believable?

9. CLIFFHANGER: Does the ending make viewers desperate to watch Episode 2? Score it from 1 to 10 and suggest how to make it stronger.

10. ON-SCREEN TEXT: Is the text readable on a small phone screen, on screen long enough, well timed, and placed inside the safe zone (not hidden by the YouTube Shorts / TikTok interface at the bottom and on the right side)?

11. VIRAL POTENTIAL: Compare this episode to the most successful revenge, humiliation and transformation short dramas on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. What do they do that this episode does not? Give a viral potential score from 1 to 10.
