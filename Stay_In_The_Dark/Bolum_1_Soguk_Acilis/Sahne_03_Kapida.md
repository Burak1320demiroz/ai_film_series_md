### Klip 3.1 – Diyafon (8 sn) · YENİ

Görsel referansları: #9 Rosewood girişi ve #4 Marco

```text
Use the attached images as reference: image 1 for the Rosewood Apartments entrance, the lighting and the color grade, image 2 for Marco (face, hair, clothing).

Photorealistic cinematic film still, 16:9 widescreen composition, medium-wide shot from the sidewalk at a slight angle. The entrance of the Rosewood Apartments building at night during a total blackout: a heavy dark wooden double door with small glass panels under a stone archway, "ROSEWOOD APARTMENTS" carved in the stone above, brass number "17" on the door frame, four stone steps with iron railings. Beside the door, an old brass intercom panel with two columns of buttons and tiny paper name labels next to each button. Marco from image 2 stands at the bottom of the steps, seen from the side, in his bright red courier jacket, not wearing the delivery box, holding a brown paper food bag in his left hand, looking up at the dark door. He is lit only by faint moonlight and the dim blood-red glow of the clouds. Every window is dark. Sinister, dread-filled atmosphere. 35mm anamorphic lens, cold blue-teal night color grade with deep blood-red accents, very high contrast, crushed blacks, subtle film grain. Keep the important elements away from the top and bottom edges of the frame.
```

Video: Başlangıç karesi bu görsel, referans olarak #4 Marco

```text
Static camera. Marco climbs the four stone steps to the door, the paper bag in his left hand. He presses several intercom buttons with his right hand, one after another, then holds one down. Nothing happens: no buzz, no light, no answer. He presses again, impatient, and mutters under his breath: "Come on, come on..." The clip ends with Marco standing still in front of the intercom panel, facing it, waiting. Keep the lighting and color grade of the first frame.

Sound: footsteps on stone steps, dead plastic clicks of the intercom buttons with no buzz, the paper bag rustling, wind. Marco's voice is low and impatient, a young American man. No music.
```

### Klip 3.5 – Fener (6 sn) · ZİNCİR ← 3.2

Görsel: Üretmiyorsun, başlangıç karesi 3.2'nin son karesi (3.3 ve 3.4 araya giren ara planlar).

Video: Referans olarak #4 Marco

```text
Static camera. Marco, holding the glowing phone in front of him, gives up waiting: the ringing stops and he ends the call with a frustrated sigh. He turns to the intercom panel and leans in close, squinting at the tiny name labels, but it is too dark to read them. He taps his phone and switches on its flashlight: a harsh, bright white beam suddenly floods the brass intercom panel and lights his face from the side, cutting sharply through the darkness. He scans the labels and mutters: "Six B... where's six B..." The clip ends with Marco holding the bright flashlight on the panel, reading. Keep the color grade of the first frame; the flashlight is the brightest thing in the whole scene.

Sound: the ringing tone cut off, a short frustrated sigh, the click of the flashlight, wind. Marco's voice is low, a young American man. No music.
```

#### Kurgu notları

Çalan telefon sahneyi birbirine bağlıyor. 3.2 ve 3.3'te çalma sesi Marco'nun hoparlöründen geliyor. 3.4'te aynı çalmayı bu kez boğuk şekilde 6. katın içinden duyuyoruz. Seyirci Mrs. Doyle'un yukarıda olduğunu ve açmadığını, Marco'dan önce anlıyor. Omni her klipte farklı bir zil sesi üretirse kurguda tek bir zil sesini üç klibin üstüne döşe, ritim aynı kalsın.

3.3'ü 3.2'nin içine koyabilirsin. Marco ekrana dokunduğu anda 3.3'e kes, sonra 3.2'ye dön, ardından 3.4'e geç. Böylece ara plan daha doğal durur.

3.5'in son karesini sakla. 4.4 YENİ bir kare, ama onu üretirken bu kareyi ışık referansı olarak vereceğiz; fenerin ışığı iki klipte aynı görünsün.

Marco'nun sesi: 2.4'teki sesi beğendiysen 3.1 ve 3.5'te ses referansı olarak vermeyi unutma.
