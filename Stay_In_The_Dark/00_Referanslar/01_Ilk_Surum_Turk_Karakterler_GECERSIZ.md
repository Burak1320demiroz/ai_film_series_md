İşte tek parça prompt:

```text
I am creating reference images for a photorealistic zombie horror short film. Generate the following 13 images as SEPARATE images, one after another, in this exact order. Do not combine them into a single image. Do not add any text, labels or logos unless specified. All characters are adults.

GLOBAL RULES FOR CHARACTER SHEETS (images 1, 3, 5, 7): The same person shown in four views side by side: front view, three-quarter view, side profile, back view. Full body, neutral relaxed standing pose. Plain medium-grey studio background, soft even lighting, photorealistic, highly detailed, identical face and clothing in every view. Wide landscape format.

GLOBAL RULES FOR FACE CLOSE-UPS (images 2, 4, 6, 8): Photorealistic head-and-shoulders portrait of the exact same person from the previous image, facing the camera, neutral expression, identical face, hair and clothing. Plain medium-grey background, soft even studio lighting, sharp focus, natural skin texture.

GLOBAL RULES FOR ZOMBIE SHEETS (images 9, 10): The same zombie shown in four views side by side: front view, three-quarter view, side profile, and a face close-up. Plain medium-grey background, soft even lighting. Photorealistic, looks like high-quality practical film makeup, not cartoonish, blood kept minimal. Wide landscape format.

GLOBAL RULES FOR FILM STILLS (images 11, 12, 13): Photorealistic cinematic film still, 35mm anamorphic lens, cold blue-teal night color grade, high contrast, deep shadows, subtle film grain, horror atmosphere. Wide landscape format.
```
